# A day with Lena

A narrated walkthrough of an account manager's day with OpenWork, alongside Open Outlook, Open Calendar, Open Drive, Open Jira and Open Confluence.

**Video:** [Watch the full film](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Y525IkWPIvZWdu6Ixf30wSve9wi03-QE/view) · 6 minutes 14 seconds.

## The day

1. **A brief already waiting.** Lena starts with a news summary in her drafts. She asks what matters for Blue Harbor and Northwind, and gets an answer tied to those accounts.
2. **How she set it up.** One sentence becomes an Automation proposal. She reviews it, clicks Create Automation, and sees it active.
3. **Organize the inbox.** OpenWork creates To do, In progress and Done folders and sorts the messages.
4. **Answer Blue Harbor.** One prompt produces a sourced, one-slide robot comparison and an unsent reply to Mateo. The film shows the reply, the saved presentation and the slide itself.
5. **Keep what worked.** Lena saves the comparison as a skill, selects it from the composer’s `/` menu for Northwind, then shares it with Nora.
6. **Prepare the account review.** A five-slide PowerPoint covers the pilots, readiness gaps, commercial forecast and decisions. All five slides appear in the film.
7. **Handle the small requests.** Create a documentation task for Nora, open an IT request for Windows login, and ask about the office dog policy. When the handbook is silent, OpenWork says so and points to People Ops.
8. **Move a meeting.** Book time with Nora and Priya, then move the same meeting to Tuesday.
9. **Close the loop.** Move the customer threads to Done, leaving drafts unsent.
10. **A live pilot app.** Build a small app from the forecast, showing units, value and status.
11. **Stay up to date.** Nora’s Activity shows Lena’s shared skill and new connector access.
